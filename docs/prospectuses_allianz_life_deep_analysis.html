9. Risk Factor Summary

  • Interest rate risk: Daily Adjustment negatively affected by rate decreases; cap increases lag rate increases
  • Derivatives used: Futures, options, swap agreements (for hedging index crediting obligations)
  • Longevity risk: GLWB and Signature Withdrawal Benefit create lifetime income guarantees
  • Lapse risk: Loans and withdrawals reduce AV and may cause policy lapse

11. Modeling Implications for KRONOS

FIA Block

  • Must model monthly sum crediting with monthly caps (0.50% guaranteed min)
  • Must model annual point-to-point with annual caps (0.25% guaranteed min)
  • Option budget drives insurer's cost of index crediting (~2% of AV)
  • GLWB rider creates tail risk: income continues when AV = $0
  • 7% rollup compounds benefit base during deferral — creates in-the-money GLWB exposure

RILA Block

  • Buffer/floor structure requires option replication pricing
  • 10% buffer = insurer absorbs first 10% of loss → embedded put spread
  • -10% floor = policyholder bears loss beyond 10% → capped downside
  • 100% buffer = full principal protection → priced via lower caps (5.6%)

ALM Considerations

  • Long duration liabilities (10-year surrender + lifetime GLWB) vs. typical 5-7yr asset portfolio
  • Negative convexity from FIA: policyholders stay in rising markets (low lapse), leave in falling markets
  • MVA provides partial interest rate hedge on surrenders during rising rate environment
  • Dynamic lapse modeling critical: spread between crediting rate and market determines lapse behavior
