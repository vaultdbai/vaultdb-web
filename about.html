Building the AI-Native Reinsurance Platform

At VaultDB, we are building KRONOS Re  the AI-native Bermuda sidecar that captures the "complexity tax" in annuity reinsurance.
Our mission is to replace legacy actuarial departments with an end-to-end, AI-driven pricing, hedging, and risk modeling platform, stripping out operational bloat and enabling Private Equity partners to deploy capital into high-yield, asset-intensive reinsurance with total algorithmic transparency.
Our vision is a world where actuarial intelligence is fully automated  where autonomous agents draft flawless deterministic models and BSCR compliance codes, and fully licensed human fiduciaries approve mathematically flawless capital flows.
