BSCR Valuation Methodology (Actuarial)

Overview

This document details the Bermuda Solvency Capital Requirement (BSCR) calculation for reinsured blocks.

Calculation Basis

The calculation follows the standard EBS (Economic Balance Sheet) approach, incorporating: 1. Market Risk Capital: VaR at 99.0% vs. 1-year horizon. 2. Long-Term Insurance Risk: Mortality and lapse stress tests.

Formulas

$$BSCR_{Total} = \sqrt{\sum_{i,j} Corr_{i,j} \times C_i \times C_j} + C_{Op}$$

Where: - $C_i$: Capital required for risk module $i$ - $Corr_{i,j}$: Component correlation matrix (BMA Standard)

Assumptions

The reporting engine defaults to the 2023 Standard Formula, but supports shocks defined in the assumptions submodule (e.g., $+15\%$ mortality increase).
