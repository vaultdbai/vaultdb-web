The Technical Edge

The KRONOS platform provides an intelligent, "Human-in-the-Loop" operational flow orchestrating complex workflows securely.

Agentic Risk Modeling

Autonomous agents continuously stress-test the liability portfolio (mortality, lapse rates, surrender behavior) against the asset portfolio (private credit, structured ABS). Agents coordinate and generate reports and deterministic code for human review.

Automated Trade Execution

Hedging strategies are executed programmatically to rebalance the portfolio 24/7, locking in the spread regardless of macro-rate volatility. "Best Practices per Product" ensure algorithms are not reinventing the wheel (e.g. Dynamic Delta/Vega for FIAs).
