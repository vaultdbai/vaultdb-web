KRONOS Re

The AI-Native Bermuda Sidecar

Capturing the "Complexity Tax" in Annuity Reinsurance. By replacing legacy actuarial departments with an end-to-end, AI-driven pricing, hedging, and risk modeling platform, we strip out operational bloat. We partner with Private Equity (PE) to provide the intelligence and regulatory framework, allowing our partners to deploy capital into high-yield, asset-intensive reinsurance with total algorithmic transparency.

Intelligent ALM & Hedging

We do not compete on distribution; we compete on math and speed. The KRONOS platform functions as a "Digital Chief Actuary," executing real-time valuation, dynamic hedging, and automated Greeks calculation. By automating the ALM-to-Reporting loop, KRONOS Re operates at a fraction of traditional OpEx, optimizing statutory capital instantly.

Bermuda Regulatory Sandbox

Designed fundamentally for the Bermuda Monetary Authority's (BMA) principles-based regime and navigating NAIC AG 55 compliance flawlessly. We employ an "Augmented" AI approach—deploying autonomous agents to draft flawless deterministic models and BSCR compliance codes, ensuring our fully licensed human fiduciaries approve mathematically flawless capital flows.

Partner With Us

The Technical Edge

The KRONOS platform provides an intelligent, "Human-in-the-Loop" operational flow orchestrating complex workflows securely. We do not build black-box neural networks; we build fully transparent agentic risk models.

Pure "Alpha" for Private Equity

A pristine, day-one environment for algorithmically ingesting a $1.5B block of Fixed Annuities or FIAs. Autonomous agents continuously stress-test the liability portfolio against the asset portfolio. Agents handle data ingestion, compile draft BSCR and STAT returns, and provide attribution analysis—leaving the final approval to a human fiduciary.

Automated Trade Execution

Hedging strategies are executed programmatically to rebalance the portfolio 24/7, locking in the spread regardless of macro-rate volatility. "Best Practices per Product" ensure algorithms are not reinventing the wheel (e.g. Dynamic Delta/Vega for FIAs, Duration Matching for Fixed Annuities).

Financial Domain Ecosystem

Deeply integrated specialized modules designed for enterprise-grade financial modeling and analytics. All modules are independently versioned and pushable to our VaultDB HUB.
