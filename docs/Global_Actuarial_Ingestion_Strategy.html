KRONOS Re: Actuarial Data Ingestion Targets

Global Regulatory & Corporate Data Matrix

To construct the ultimate stochastic ALM and Assumption models, we must scrape specific documents from the exact endpoints where insurers lodge their product definitions and capital requirements. Actuarial product data exists across three distinct layers: Securities, State Insurance, and Offshore Reinsurance.

1. US Securities Filings (SEC EDGAR)

Target Market: Variable Annuities (VAs), Registered Index-Linked Annuities (RILAs), Variable Universal Life (VUL).
URL Source: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK[...].json (API) | https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/companysearch Key Target Forms: * Form N-4 & N-6: Core prospectuses for VAs and VULs containing precise fee tables and GLWB guarantees. * Form S-1 & S-3: Core prospectuses for RILAs and MYGAs involving downside tail risk. * Form 424B2 / 424B3 / 497: Mandatory pricing supplements releasing the live declared "Cap Rates", "Participation Rates", and "Option Budgets".

2. US State Insurance Regulators (NAIC SERFF)

Target Market: Traditional Fixed, Fixed Indexed Annuities (FIAs), Whole Life. (Exempt from SEC registration).
URL Source: https://serff.com | Individual State DOIs (e.g., Texas DOI, NY DFS Public Tracking).
Key Target Forms: * Actuarial Memorandum: The most valuable internal document detailing the exact assumption matrices (lapse arrays, expected margins, mortality). * Policy Forms / Specimen Contracts: Defines Surrender Charge penalties and MVA (Market Value Adjustment) schedules.

3. Offshore Reinsurance Regulators (Bermuda BMA & Cayman CIMA)

Target Market: Block Life Reinsurance Entities (Athene Life Re, Fortitude Re, Venerable).
URL Source: https://www.bma.bm/public-reports/financial-condition-report
Key Target Forms: * Financial Condition Report (FCR): A mandatory BMA public disclosure mapping offshore block acquisition assumptions, risk profiles, and capital reserve limits against the Bermuda Solvency Capital Requirement (BSCR).

4. European Regulators (Solvency II Regime)

Target Market: UK and European Parents of US life cos (Legal & General, Prudential plc).
URL Source: UK Companies House | EIOPA Registers | EU Corporate Sites.
Key Target Forms: * Solvency and Financial Condition Report (SFCR): Details Solvency II's Best Estimate Liabilities (BEL) curves and Risk Margins.

5. Corporate Intermediary Portals

Target Market: Current declared and consumer-facing literature.
URL Source: Public /annuities/prospectuses/ domains (e.g., Athene, Prudential, Corebridge).
Key Target Forms: * Corporate Agent Guides & Brochures: Glossy PDFs and weekly excel rate sheets required to calibrate real-time pricing grids.
